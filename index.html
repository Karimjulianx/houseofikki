The Name

"Iki" comes from Japanese and describes a form of understated elegance: reduced, precise, intentional. "Ikki" carries a sense of acting in one continuous flow, with full presence and focus, historically reflecting a collective energy: a quiet determination to move forward. IKKI merges both meanings into a new interpretation: controlled intensity.

IKKI is a method of movement.

Rooted in Pilates, redefined through light, breath and control, designed to work the body beyond the visible.

Not softer. Not louder. More precise.

A members club for those who seek awareness over repetition, discipline over distraction, and connection that goes deeper than the surface.

Welcome to IKKI.

Our Concept

Austria's First Red Light Recovery Studio.

House of IKKI was developed together with Luminous Labs, our premium partner in light-based recovery technology. The result is a concept built on scientifically proven methods and effects, a first for Austria, and unlike anything else on the market.

Every Pilates, Yoga, Breathwork and Sound Healing class is paired with medical-grade Red Light Therapy, designed to support recovery, vitality and long-term health, turning every session into more than a workout.
